This notice explains what personal information Active Labs collects when you use this website or join our waitlist, why we collect it, what we do with it, and the rights you have over it. We have written it in plain English rather than legal boilerplate, because a notice you cannot understand does not do its job.

Active Labs is not yet open. We are not currently providing clinical services, and we do not hold anyone's test results or medical records. Today this notice covers one thing: the details people give us to join the waitlist, or to enquire about a programme for their team.

2 · What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Your name and email address. Required — without these we cannot add you to the list or tell you when we open.
  • Your phone number. Optional.
  • The interests you tick — for example "Baseline testing", "Hormone health" or "Metabolic / weight". Optional. Some of these tell us something about your health, and we treat them with extra care. See section 4.
  • Your preferred consultation format, and how you heard about us. Optional.
  • Anything you write in the free-text box. Optional. We ask you not to put symptoms or medical details here.
  • Your consent choices, and the date and time you gave them. We keep this as our record that you agreed.

When you enquire about a team programme

  • Your name, work email, company name, team size, preferred delivery format, the programme options you tick, and anything you write in the free-text box. These are business details, not health information.

When you register interest in activewear or a community event

  • Your first name, your email address, and which thing you registered for — the shop, the run club, or a coffee morning. Nothing else, and none of it is health information.

When you simply browse the site

  • We run no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind on this website. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not know who you are unless you tell us.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

4 · Health information gets extra protection

If you tick an interest such as "Hormone health" or "Metabolic / weight" on a clinic waitlist, UK data protection law treats that as special category data — information about your health. It carries stricter rules than an ordinary name and email.

Because of that:

  • Ticking those boxes is entirely optional. You can join the waitlist without ticking any of them, and it will not affect your place in the queue or how we treat you.
  • We ask for your separate, explicit consent before storing them. That is the tick box directly above the "Join the waitlist" button — it is unticked by default, and we never tick it for you.
  • We use these interests only to shape what we tell you about, and to understand overall what the waitlist is interested in. We do not use them to make any decision about you, and there is no automated decision-making or profiling.
  • You can ask us to delete them while keeping your waitlist place. Just email us.

7 · How long we keep it

  • Waitlist entries: 12 months from the date you join, or until you ask us to remove you — whichever comes first. If we have not opened by then, we will ask whether you want to stay on the list rather than quietly keep your details.
  • Team enquiries: 12 months from your last contact with us.
  • Shop and event interest lists: 12 months from the date you register, or until you unsubscribe — whichever comes first.
  • Consent records: for as long as we hold the information they relate to, plus a short period afterwards, so we can show we had your permission.

When a retention period ends, we delete the information.

8 · Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See what we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Have it deleted. Because we rely on your consent, you can ask for this at any time and we will act on it.
  • Withdraw your consent — for the health interests, for the emails, or for everything. Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link, and you can email us at any point.
  • Restrict or object to what we do with it.
  • Take it with you in a portable format.

To use any of these, email hello@activelabs.health. We will respond within one month, and it costs you nothing.

9 · Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking.

If you switch the site to dark mode, we save that single preference in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. It stays on your device, it is never sent to us, and it contains nothing that identifies you. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

10 · Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information — particularly when we open and begin providing clinical services — we will update this notice and change the date at the top. Where a change materially affects you, we will tell you directly rather than rely on you noticing.
