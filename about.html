The UK's longevity sector is concentrated almost entirely in London, serving the wealthy few. Active Labs will bring genuine, science-led preventive medicine to Brighton & Hove — aspirational, but accessible.

Our position

Above the GP and the spa. Below the London luxury club.

We sit deliberately in the middle: more rigorous than a wellness spa with a drip menu, more personal and preventive than a rushed private GP, and far more accessible than the ultra-premium longevity clubs serving high-net-worth Londoners.

The brand should feel scientific and credible, warm and human, empowering, locally rooted, and aspirational — the clinic where founders, athletes and health-conscious professionals go.

Scientific

Data-first, evidence-based, clinician-led. Every recommendation stands on published science.

Human

A partner in your health, not a cold clinical transaction. We give you time and attention.

Empowering

Your health data belongs to you. We hand you agency, understanding and a clear plan.

Local

Rooted in Brighton & Hove's culture and community — being built here, for here.
