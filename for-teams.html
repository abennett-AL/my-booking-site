How the programme works

What a team programme looks like

How we design a team programme to run — from baseline testing day, through a twelve-week protocol, to retest and an aggregate report.

BASELINE
Day one

An onsite testing day, or our national lab partners where a team is spread out. 70+ biomarkers per person, so everyone starts from a genuine clinical baseline rather than a tick-box screening.

PROTOCOL
12 weeks

Each person gets an individual protocol after their consultation, designed to be worked through over a twelve-week cycle before a retest shows what has moved.

REPORTING
Aggregate only

After each cycle the organisation receives an anonymised, group-level report — designed to surface early metabolic and cardiovascular risk across the team without ever identifying an individual.
